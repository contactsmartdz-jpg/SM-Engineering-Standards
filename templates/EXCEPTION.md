# Engineering Standards Exception — Template

> An exception documents a risk decision. It does not change failed test results into successes.

- Exception ID: [EX-YYYY-NNN]
- Rule ID(s): [e.g. SEC-07, QG-05]
- Project / affected scope: [service, component, environment, revision]
- Requester: [role]
- Accountable risk owner: [human/authorized governance role]
- Reason the standard cannot be met now: [specific evidence]
- Impact and threat analysis: [affected users, data, failure modes]
- Alternatives considered: [options and why not selected]
- Compensating safeguards: [controls and validation evidence]
- Decision: [approved / denied / pending]
- Decision date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
- Expiration date: [YYYY-MM-DD; required]
- Reassessment trigger: [date, release, security event, etc.]
- Remediation plan / tracking reference: [link]
- Evidence: [tests, scans, independent review, incident records]
- Approver signature or traceable approval link: [reference]

**Non-negotiable:** The exception must be authorized before a prohibited action, must be specific and time-bounded, and must not hide test failures, legal violations, or missing authorization. Some obligations are not waivable.
